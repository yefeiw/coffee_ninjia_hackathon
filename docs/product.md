# Product Doc

## Vision
Coffee Ninja helps community members quickly find useful professional conversations.

## Problem
Potentially valuable introductions often fail due to low signal, limited organizer bandwidth, and weak follow-through.

## MVP
- Intake profile
- AI-assisted structuring
- Explainable matching
- Intro + activity recommendation
- Lightweight feedback loop

## Demo Success Criteria
- Intent captured
- Matches ranked
- Reasons clear
- Actionable handoff to conversation
